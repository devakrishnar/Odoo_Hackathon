## 1. Overview & Architecture

The Customer Self-Service Portal enables Brightloop Software subscribers to independently manage recurring subscriptions, browse products and pricing plans, request quotations, view tax-compliant invoices, download official invoice PDFs, record and track payment history, and update their account credentials.

### Architectural Principles
- **Strict Customer Identity Derivation**: All customer data operations derive customer identity directly from the verified JWT access token (`req.user.id`). No customer identity is accepted via browser route parameters or request bodies to prevent unauthorized horizontal access.
- **Role Isolation**: Only accounts with the `CUSTOMER` role can access the portal. Internal `ADMIN` and `STAFF` users are prevented from entering the customer portal by route guards.
- **Real-Time Data Integration**: No mock data or hardcoded statistics are used. All metrics (active subscriptions, next billing renewal date, outstanding balances, payment settlements) are retrieved live from the shared backend and PostgreSQL database.

## 3. Customer Portal Feature Guide

### 1. Authentication & Security
- **Registration (`/register`)**: Customer account creation enforcing email uniqueness and strict password requirements (>8 characters, at least 1 uppercase, 1 lowercase, 1 special character). Automatically provisions linked `Customer` record in PostgreSQL.
- **Login (`/login`)**: Secure JWT sign-in with role verification and quick demo credential filler.
- **Forgot Password (`/forgot-password`)**: Generates email instructions and development reset token.
- **Reset Password (`/reset-password`)**: Token-verified password reset enforcing password policy.
- **Route Guard (`ProtectedRoute`)**: Rejects non-customer roles (`ADMIN`, `STAFF`) with an access-restricted screen.

### 2. Live Dashboard (`/`)
- Active plan summary card and lifecycle status (`ACTIVE`, `TRIALING`, etc.).
- Next billing renewal date calculated from active subscription `currentPeriodEnd`.
- Outstanding unpaid invoice balance calculated from open invoices.
- Total settled payments aggregated from live transactions.
- Quick summary tables for recent invoices and recent payments.

### 3. Product Catalog & Subscription Plans (`/plans`)
- Dynamic catalog fetched from `/api/products` and `/api/plans`.
- Filterable by billing cycle (Monthly / Annual / All Cycles).
- **Quotation / Plan Request**: Quantity and seat configuration, estimated total calculation, and submission to `/api/quotations/request`.

### 4. My Subscriptions & Offers (`/subscriptions`)
- Subscription cards with status badges (`Draft`, `Quotation`, `Confirmed`, `Active`, `Closed`, `Paused`, `Past Due`).
- Full line-item breakdown drawer.
- **Quotations Tab**: Review quotation offers issued by sales staff with 1-click **"Accept Quotation"** (`POST /api/quotations/:id/accept`).

### 5. My Invoices (`/invoices`)
- Complete invoice list with issue date, due date, subtotal, tax, discount, total amount, amount paid, and outstanding balance.
- Search and status filters (`ISSUED`, `PARTIALLY_PAID`, `PAID`, `OVERDUE`).
- **PDF Download**: Streams tax-compliant PDF invoice generated by PDFKit on the backend via `/api/invoices/:id/pdf`.
- Detailed breakdown modal with item lines, applied taxes, and eligible discounts.

### 6. Payment History & Settlements (`/payments`)
- Overview of total settled revenue, outstanding balance, and settlement policy.
- Transaction ledger showing payment numbers, payment methods, transaction notes, dates, and associated invoice references.
- **"Make a Payment" Modal**: Select an outstanding invoice, specify payment method, enter payment amount, and submit to `/api/payments`. Atomically updates invoice balance and status in PostgreSQL.

### 7. Customer Profile & Security (`/profile`)
- View and update permitted profile information: Company name, phone number, address, city, state, postal code, country, tax ID.
- Change password with current password verification and password policy compliance.

## 4. API Endpoints & Payloads Specification

| Method | Endpoint | Description | Scoping & Ownership |
|---|---|---|---|
| `POST` | `/api/auth/register` | Customer sign-up | Creates `User` and `Customer` record |
| `POST` | `/api/auth/login` | Authenticate customer | Returns JWT token with customer payload |
| `POST` | `/api/auth/forgot-password` | Request password reset token | Scoped to registered email |
| `POST` | `/api/auth/reset-password` | Update password via token | Validates token and updates hash |
| `GET` | `/api/auth/me` | Fetch authenticated session | Scoped by JWT |
| `GET` | `/api/products` | Browse product catalog | Read access |
| `GET` | `/api/plans` | Browse subscription tiers | Read access |
| `GET` | `/api/subscriptions` | Customer's subscriptions | Scoped to authenticated customer |
| `GET` | `/api/subscriptions/:id` | Subscription line items & terms | Ownership checked |
| `GET` | `/api/invoices` | Customer's invoice list | Scoped to authenticated customer |
| `GET` | `/api/invoices/:id` | Invoice details & line items | Ownership checked |
| `GET` | `/api/invoices/:id/pdf` | Download official PDF invoice | Ownership checked |
| `GET` | `/api/payments` | Customer's payment history | Scoped to authenticated customer |
| `POST` | `/api/payments` | Settle / record payment on invoice | Ownership checked, updates balance |
| `GET` | `/api/quotations` | Customer's quotation requests | Scoped to authenticated customer |
| `POST` | `/api/quotations/request` | Submit new quotation request | Scoped to authenticated customer |
| `POST` | `/api/quotations/:id/accept` | Accept an issued quotation | Ownership checked |
| `GET` | `/api/customers/profile` | Retrieve customer profile | Scoped to authenticated customer |
| `PUT` | `/api/customers/profile` | Update contact & billing fields | Scoped to authenticated customer |
| `POST` | `/api/customers/change-password` | Update account password | Scoped to authenticated customer |
